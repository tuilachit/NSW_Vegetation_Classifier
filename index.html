Open ELVIS Portal

Ordering LiDAR from ELVIS

  1. Click Open ELVIS Portal to visit elevation.fsdf.org.au.
  2. Click Order Data in the top navigation bar.
  3. Paste your bounding box using the Copy Coordinates button above.
  4. Select Point Cloud as product type, LAS / LAZ as format.
  5. Add tiles to cart and complete your order. A download link will arrive by email.
  6. When your .zip archive arrives, return here and proceed to Step 3.
LiDAR orders may take 24–48 hours. You can close this page and return later — your uploaded aerial image will need to be re-uploaded.
